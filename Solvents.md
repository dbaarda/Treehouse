# Solvents Oils and Waxes

This is a summary of various solvents, oils, and waxes, and what they can be
used for.

## Waxes

Properties: Solid at room temp, low melting point, don't evaporate, burn slowly.

## Oils

Properties: Liquid at room temp, high boiling point, don't evaporate (much), burn well.

* paraffin (paraffinic distillate AKA liquid wax, mineral oil) $16.95/l -
  doesn't evaporate, can thin waxes, can be food safe.
* linseed oil - "drying oil" v-high unsaturated but oxidation cross-links into solid.
* coconut - v-high saturated, v-v-low unsaturated, med smoke point. Only just
  solid at room temp?
* avocado - v-high mono-saturated, low unsaturated, v-high smoke point
* canola - v-high mono-saturated, high smoke point
* olive - v-high mono-saturated, v-low unsaturated, high smoke point
* rice-bran - mixed saturation, v-high smoke point, used to make furnature wax?
* peanut - high mono-saturated, v-high smoke point.
* palm - high saturated, v-low unsaturated, v-high smoke point.
* sunflower - v-high mono-saturated, v-high smoke point.

## Solvents

Properties: Liquid at room temp, low boiling point, evaporate fast, burn very fast.

Most of these are quite toxic and can emit toxic vapors. Some of them are not
even legaly availble in some regions.

* Acetone - strong solvent, fast evaporation, removes superglue
* Toluene - very strong solvent, highly flamable, fast evaporation, insoluble in
  water.
* Xylene (Xylene) - very strong solvent, mid-to-slow evaporation.
* white spirits (refined Naptha heavy) - strong solvent, mid evaporation, leaves no residue.
* Mineral Turpentine (dirty Naptha light+heavy) - even stronger solvent, fast evaporation 
* Low Odor Turpentine (less dirty Naptha heavy) - same as white spirits?
* Pure Gum Turpentine (plant gum turpentine) - art solvent, eucyliptus odor?
* Water Based Turpentine (2-Butoxyethanol+water) - cleaning solvent, not suitable as paint thinner.
* Kerosene (Naptha, heavy) -
* Methylated Spirits Methanol- fast evaporation, cleaning solvent, fuel
* Ethanol - like Methanol, but slower evaporation?
* Isopropal Alchohol IPA - fast evaporation, medium solvent, water soluable, disinfectant.
* Methyl Ethyl Ketone MEK - very fast evaporation, strong solvent, water soluable.


## Combinations

Hard waxes can be melted and mixed with oils to make them softer. Mixing Wax
with coconut oil should make a decent wood-polishing wax.
